The project format

Every project tells a making story

  1. 1

    Concept

    What you're building and why — the sketch, the inspiration, the problem you're solving.

  2. 2

    Materials

    A clear bill of materials with quantities and where to source them locally.

  3. 3

    Build log

    Step-by-step photos and notes — including the mistakes, which are often the best part.

  4. 4

    Reveal

    The finished piece, what you'd do differently, and the remix invitation.

Project tools

Built for long-form making

  • Versioned steps

    Readers can suggest corrections to any step; authors approve edits in one tap.

  • Comment threads

    Questions land right under the step they're about — no more scrolling to find answers.

  • Remix system

    Build on someone's project and publish a remix that credits the original automatically.

  • Materials tables

    Structured bills of materials that readers can copy straight into their shopping list.

Project FAQ

Publishing questions

How detailed do projects need to be?

Three or more steps with photos is the community minimum — the best projects read like a friend explaining over tea.

Can I publish a project I already shared elsewhere?

Yes, as long as it's your work. Reposts of others' projects are removed.

What is a remix?

A project that builds on another maker's work, with credit and links to the original. Remixes are a badge of honour here.

Do projects have to be finished?

No — 'WIP log' projects are welcome and often get the most engaged comments.
