Community FAQ

How communities work

Can I start my own community?

Yes — any member can start a community for a craft or niche that doesn't have a home yet. You become its first moderator.

What are the global rules?

No hate, no harassment, no stolen work, no spam. Everything else is decided by each community's own rules.

How do build-alongs work?

A moderator announces a theme and timeline; members post progress in a pinned thread and celebrate finishes together.

Can communities be private?

Yes — invite-only communities exist for guilds, studios, and workshops that want a private space.
