Contests

Craft it. Enter it. Win it.

Monthly themed contests with community judging, prize pools, and the kind of bragging rights that stick to a workshop wall.

See this month's theme

Why contests

The engagement engine

  • Monthly themes

    From 'Zero Waste' to 'Festival Light' — a fresh brief every month to stretch your skills.

  • Community judging

    Shortlisted entries are voted on by members, with a makers' jury for the final call.

  • Prize pools

    Cash and gear prizes from local sponsors — woodworking tools, pottery kits, yarn hauls.

  • Deadline pressure

    A real deadline is the best studio assistant. Ask any maker who's won one.

How to enter

Three steps to the podium

  1. 1

    Post your project

    Publish a project or WIP log and tag it with the current contest theme.

  2. 2

    Show the process

    Entries with honest build logs score higher with the jury — process photos matter.

  3. 3

    Share for votes

    The community votes in the final week. Campaign kindly; the jury watches for brigade tactics.
