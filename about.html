About

Made by makers, for makers

Nepal's handicraft sector has employed generations of skilled hands — pashmina alone was 82% of handicraft exports in the early 2000s. We built the machine to keep that momentum moving online.

Our story

From scattered groups to one machine

Nepali makers sold through scattered Facebook groups and handicraft stores, while global makers lived on Reddit, Ravelry, and Instructables. Crafting Machines gives every maker one home — feed, community, tutorials, and shop together.

  • Born in Patan, where thangka studios and metal workshops share courtyards
  • 380+ communities and growing across 60+ countries [CLIENT-COPY: confirm with client]
  • Nepali and English first-class languages
  • Maker-owned: no ads in the feed, ever

What we believe

The maker's code

  • Hands first

    Process photos, honest mistakes, and real materials — the feed values the making, not just the reveal.

  • Generosity

    Projects are gifts to the community. Credit is automatic, remixing is encouraged, and stealing is banned.

  • Kindness by design

    Rules written up front, mods backed by a global team, and a code of conduct every member signs.

  • Roots & reach

    Proud of Nepal's craft heritage and open to the world's makers — the machine runs both ways.
