Marketplace FAQ

Buying and selling questions

What can I sell?

Finished handmade work and craft supplies. Re-sold factory goods belong elsewhere — the marketplace is for makers.

How do payouts work?

Nepali sellers are paid via eSewa, Khalti, or bank transfer within 3 working days of delivery confirmation.

What if a buyer receives a damaged item?

Photo evidence within 48 hours triggers a full refund from our buyer protection — no questions beyond the photos.

Can I sell internationally?

Yes — international shipping labels are generated at checkout, with customs guidance for fragile handmade goods.

Your work is worth selling.

List your first piece free — the feed will already know your name by the time it sells.
