Common questions

My project was removed. Why?

Most removals are reposts of others' work or missing attribution. Reply to the notice and we'll review it with you.

How do I become a verified seller?

Apply from your profile — a human reviews your listing photos and background within 3 working days.

Can brands sponsor a contest?

Yes — we love local tool, yarn, and clay sponsors. Email partnerships@craftingmachines.com.

Do you moderate in Nepali?

Yes — our mod team covers Nepali and English, and several communities run fully bilingual.

Prefer to show, not tell?

Skip the form — the fastest way to reach the community is a project post.
