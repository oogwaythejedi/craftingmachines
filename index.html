Why Crafting Machines

A feed, a workshop, and a shop in one

Craft communities are scattered across Facebook groups and Instagram. We gave makers one machine.

  • Maker feed

    Hot, new, and top — a Reddit-style feed of WIPs, wins, and questions from makers you follow.

  • Communities

    Topic hubs for fiber, wood, clay, metal, paper, paint, and digital fabrication — each with its own rules and mods.

  • Project tutorials

    Step-by-step project format with materials lists, photos, and comments — the Instructables model, community-run.

  • Marketplace

    Sell finished work and craft supplies with ratings, reviews, and verified sellers.

  • Contests

    Monthly themed contests with community judging — the engagement engine that brings makers back.

  • Moderation & safety

    Explicit community rules, mod tools, and a code of kindness — trust scaffolding that Reddit groups dream of.

Questions

Before you join the machine

What kinds of craft are welcome?

All of them — fiber, wood, clay, metal, paper, paint, digital fabrication, cosplay, food crafts. If you make it by hand, it belongs here.

Is Crafting Machines free?

Posting, communities, and projects are free. The marketplace takes a small commission on sales, and premium contests have optional entries.

How are communities moderated?

Each community writes its own rules, and our global mod team backs them up. Every report gets a human review.

Can I sell my work?

Yes — the marketplace is open to verified makers. Verification is free and takes a few days.

Is there a Nepali-language space?

Yes — several communities run bilingual Nepali and English threads, and the platform ships with both languages.
